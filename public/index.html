Study Portal — Standards-Aligned Practice for Kindergarten Through Grade 12

Study Portal is a free practice library for students, teachers and families. It covers mathematics, English language arts, science, social studies and world languages across every grade band, with diagnostic placement, spaced review and printable progress reports. Every skill is mapped to Common Core State Standards, the Next Generation Science Standards, or the relevant state framework, and every question ships with a step-by-step explanation rather than a bare answer key.

Mathematics

Kindergarten through grade 2 builds counting, place value and addition and subtraction within 100. Grades 3 to 5 cover multiplication and division fluency, equivalent fractions, decimals, area, volume and coordinate graphing. Middle school moves through ratios, integers, linear equations and the Pythagorean theorem, and high school tracks run from Algebra I through AP Calculus with worked solutions for quadratics, logarithms, trigonometric identities, limits, derivatives and integrals.

English Language Arts

Early literacy begins with phonemic awareness and decoding, then develops close reading of literary and informational text, author's purpose, figurative language and evidence-based analysis. Writing moves from sentence construction to narrative, expository, argumentative and research essays with MLA and APA citation, while grammar practice covers parts of speech, agreement, clauses and punctuation.

Science & Social Studies

Science spans habitats, weather and the water cycle in elementary grades; cells, plate tectonics, chemical reactions and the solar system in middle school; and full Biology, Chemistry and Physics in high school. Social studies runs from ancient civilisations and early United States history through civics, geography, economics and personal financial literacy.

World Languages & Test Preparation

Courses in Spanish, French, German, Italian, Mandarin, Japanese and more are mapped to CEFR bands A1–C1 and ACTFL levels. Preparation tracks cover the SAT and ACT, AP examinations, and language certifications including DELE, DELF, Goethe-Zertifikat, HSK and JLPT, with timed practice sections and per-topic score breakdowns.
